## Purpose

The Monthly Leadership Review is the principal executive coaching report generated by the Leadership Nexus Alignment Companion (LNAC).

It is designed to transform four to five weeks of participant check-ins into a concise, evidence-based leadership briefing that prepares a coach for a focused, meaningful coaching conversation.

The report is intended to answer one fundamental question:

> "What is most important for this coach and participant to discuss this month?"

The report should never feel like an analytics dashboard or a scorecard.

It should feel like an executive briefing.

---

## Primary Audience

The primary audience is the Leadership Nexus coach.

Secondary audiences may include:

- The participant
- An authorized organizational sponsor
- Leadership Nexus administrators

Every design and content decision should prioritize the needs of the coach.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

The Monthly Leadership Review exists to support better coaching conversations.

Its purpose is not to present data.

Its purpose is to transform data into insight.

Every section of the report should help the coach:

- Understand the participant's current leadership state
- Recognize meaningful leadership patterns
- Distinguish strengths from emerging risks
- Identify developmental opportunities
- Leave the meeting with clear next actions

---

## Relationship to LNAC

The Monthly Leadership Review is one component of the Leadership Nexus Alignment Companion.

- **Weekly Check-Ins** generate participant observations.
- **The inference engine** analyzes those observations.
- **The Monthly Leadership Review** communicates the most meaningful conclusions.

The report is therefore an executive coaching deliverable — not a raw analytical output.

---

## Relationship to the Executive Design Language

The report will follow the Leadership Nexus Executive Design Language (EDL).

- The EDL defines **how** information is visually communicated.
- This document defines **why** the information exists.
- Future functional specifications will define **what** each section is responsible for communicating.

---

## Canonical Architecture

The approved Monthly Leadership Review mock-up represents the canonical report architecture.

Future development should preserve the overall information hierarchy while continuously improving:

- Typography
- Spacing
- Readability
- Visual hierarchy
- Chart quality
- PDF presentation

Major structural changes should require explicit approval.

The approved information flow is:

1. **Context** — Header, client information, executive summary
2. **Insight** — Leadership alignment snapshot, what's working, watch out for
3. **Evidence** — Leadership profile distribution, weekly alignment trend
4. **Coaching Priorities** — Primary focus and explanation
5. **Development Actions** — Growth recommendation, secondary focus

---

## Measures of Success

The Monthly Leadership Review is successful when a coach can answer the following questions within five minutes:

- What changed this month?
- What is working well?
- Where is the participant drifting?
- How well are Thought and Action aligned?
- Is leadership momentum improving or declining?
- What is the highest coaching priority?
- What developmental practice should be encouraged next?
